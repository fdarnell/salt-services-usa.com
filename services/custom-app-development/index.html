Custom app development, from $10,000

Most small businesses run on five subscriptions and a lot of copying things by hand. When none of the off-the-shelf tools fit the way your business actually works, we write one that does. Custom apps start at $10,000, quoted flat and in writing before we start.

Tell us what you need built

What does a custom app cost?

Custom apps start at $10,000. That's the floor for a working app with logins, saved records and the screens your team uses every day. What moves the price above that:

  • How many screens and steps the job has
  • How many kinds of people log in: owner, office, crew, customers
  • Connections to other software, like a CRM, a payment processor or texting
  • Moving your existing records in from spreadsheets or an old system
  • An App Store version, if you need one on top of the browser version

You get the build price in writing, along with anything that runs monthly after launch, before we write a line of code. If a smaller tool on your website would fix the problem for a lot less, we'll say so.

What kind of app makes sense?

The best ones start with a job somebody does by hand every week. Writing up estimates at the kitchen table at night. Re-typing the same customer into three systems. Booking rentals over the phone. Chasing signatures by email. If you can describe the steps, we can usually build them, and the app pays for itself in hours your team gets back.

It doesn't have to be a big job. It has to be a job that comes up every week.

How a custom app build works

  • We sit down with whoever does the work today and watch how it's done, paper and all
  • You get a written quote: what the app does, what it costs, and when you'll see it
  • We build the part that saves the most time first, so your team is using it early
  • Your team tries it on real jobs and tells us what's wrong, and we fix it
  • Launch, training, and a check-in after the first few weeks

Custom app questions, answered straight

How much does a custom app cost?

Custom apps start at $10,000. Where a build lands above that depends on how many screens it has, how many kinds of people log in, and what it has to connect to. We quote it flat and in writing after we've seen how you work, so you know the number before we write any code.

Why does a custom app cost more than a website?

A website mostly shows information. An app does work: it does the math, stores your customers' records, sends texts, takes signatures and payments, and has to be right every time someone taps a button. That's more to build and a lot more to test.

Can you just add a feature to software we already use?

Sometimes, if the software lets outside tools connect to it. We check what it allows before we promise anything. If a small tool on your website would solve the problem, we'll tell you that instead of selling you a whole app.

Will it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Everything we build works in the browser on a computer, an iPhone, an iPad or an Android phone. The estimating app on this page is used on a tablet in customers' driveways. Ask us about an App Store version if you need one; our own calendar app is headed there now.

Who fixes it when something needs changing?

We do. The people who wrote it are the people who answer the phone, so a change doesn't start with a support ticket. Every quote spells out what's included after launch.

Do you build apps for businesses outside East Tennessee?

Yes. Most of the work happens on a screen share anyway. Owners in Sevier, Knox, Blount and Jefferson counties usually get an in-person sit-down to map out the job, and everyone else gets the same thing over video.
