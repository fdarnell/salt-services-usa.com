How far a good business is felt
01 / 05 · Behind the bench

The shop.

Felt first by the people who work there. Steady hours, and a boss who shows up.

02 / 05 · Next door

The street.

Then by the neighbors. Your customers park out front and wander into the shop beside you.

03 / 05 · Where people gather

The square.

Then wherever folks meet. Your name on the banner, your cookies at the fall festival.

04 / 05 · Sevier County

The county.

Then down the road. People drive past two closer places because somebody told them about yours.

05 / 05 · East Tennessee

The region.

Then further than you’ll ever drive. A name passed along the valley by people you’ll never meet.
