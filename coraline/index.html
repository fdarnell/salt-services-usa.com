What's inside

Most small businesses glue four or five apps together and still copy things by hand. Coraline is one system, and we can change it when your business needs something it doesn't do yet.

  • One inbox

    Website forms, calls, texts, email and Google reviews in one list, answered from your business number on a laptop or a phone.

  • Follow-up that runs itself

    A missed call gets a text back within seconds. Follow-ups go out by text and email, and reminders keep coming back until someone makes the call.

  • Pipeline and calendar

    Every job from new lead to won, with the next step on every card and estimate visits on a shared calendar.

  • Contracts and reviews

    Customers sign on their phone. When the job's done, one tap sends a review request, with up to two polite reminders.

  • A built-in assistant

    Ask it who's waiting on a callback and it answers from your own records. It also answers chats on your website and hands off to a person.

  • Reports

    Where your leads came from and what's still open, without exporting a spreadsheet.

Coraline questions, answered

Do I have to set Coraline up myself?

No. We set it up for you: a pipeline built around how you sell, your contacts moved over, your website forms and business number connected, and your team trained. A simple setup can go live in as little as three days.

What does Coraline cost?

There are three plans: Starter at $99 a month, Growth at $297 and Pro at $497, with a one-time setup of $1,000, $1,500 or $2,500. Paying for a year up front gets you two months free. Every plan includes some texting and calling each month. Anything past that comes out of a prepaid balance, and you'll have both numbers in writing before we start.

Will my customers see the name Coraline?

No. Your texts and emails go out under your business name, from your business number. Your customers only ever see you.

Does Coraline work on a phone?

Yes. It runs on a laptop, an iPhone or an iPad, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. A version for the App Store is on the way.

Will Coraline work with my existing website?

Yes. Coraline's forms and website chat go on any site, including ones we didn't build. Every form fill and chat lands in the same inbox as your calls and texts.

Do I need Coraline to work with Salt Services?

No. Our web design, SEO and advertising work stands on its own. Coraline is what we recommend when a business needs a CRM, because we wrote it and we run our own business on it.
